# Şehir haritası görsel promptları (ChatGPT)

Toplam 10 görsel: 5 harita arka planı + 5 oyun zemini.
Mahalle ve Memleket görselleriyle aynı stilde olsun diye her prompt aynı kalıpla başlıyor.
Bitince dosyaları bana at, ben boyutlarını oyuna uydurup eklerim (harita 1080×4476, zemin dikişsiz).

---

## A) Harita arka planları (dikey, 2:3)

Her birine ortak giriş (başına yapıştır):

> Top-down bird's-eye view illustration for a mobile game map background, vertical portrait 2:3.
> Cozy, warm, hand-painted stylized look with soft lighting and gentle shadows, like a premium casual game.
> The CENTER of the image (about 60% of the width) must stay EMPTY and calm (plain ground only), because
> game level buttons and a winding path will be placed there. All details and props only along the LEFT
> and RIGHT edges. No text, no letters, no logos, no people, no cars driving in the middle, no UI.

**1. HaritaSehirMerkezi — Şehir Merkezi**
> Ground: grey asphalt with a clean pale sidewalk strip. Edges: a bus stop with a small shelter, traffic
> lights, a zebra crossing at the very top edge, round metal manhole covers, low curbstones, potted trees,
> a red fire hydrant, a few pigeons, a newspaper kiosk. Morning light, soft blue tint.

**2. HaritaSanayi — Sanayi**
> Ground: light concrete with cracks and a few dark oil stains. Edges: an auto repair shop entrance,
> stacked tires, a small forklift, wooden pallets, oil barrels, a sand pile with a shovel, scrap metal,
> a yellow-black striped barrier, an open manhole with cones around it. Warm noon light.

**3. HaritaLiman — Liman**
> Ground: wet stone quay with puddle reflections. Edges: a ferry pier on one side with blue water, mooring
> bollards with thick ropes, coiled ropes, fish crates with ice, a small fishing boat, seagulls on posts,
> a small lighthouse at the top corner, a gentle concrete ramp. Cool morning light.

**4. HaritaAvm — AVM**
> Ground: shiny cream marble tiles with soft reflections. Edges: shop windows with colorful awnings,
> an escalator corner, a small fountain with a wet floor around it, a fruit stand with watermelons,
> potted palms, benches, shopping bags, a cinema poster frame (blank). Bright warm indoor light.

**5. HaritaGokdelen — Gökdelenler**
> Ground: dark polished stone plaza. Edges: rooftops of glass skyscrapers seen from above, a helipad
> circle (no letters) in one corner, rooftop gardens, glowing windows, string lights, small water
> features. Evening blue hour with warm city lights glowing, slightly purple sky reflections.

---

## B) Oyun zeminleri — GERÇEKÇİ (oyun içinde misketlerin altında görünen yer)

Her birine ortak giriş (başına yapıştır):

> Photorealistic seamless tileable ground texture, shot from directly above with a camera held parallel
> to the ground (perfect orthographic top-down, no perspective). Square 1:1, very high detail, real
> material photographed in soft overcast daylight: natural micro-detail, tiny imperfections, subtle
> color variation, NO strong shadows, NO objects, NO text, NO painted lines, NO borders, NO vignette.
> Must tile seamlessly on all four sides. The surface should be calm enough that a white chalk circle
> and small glass marbles stay clearly visible on top of it.

1. **SehirMerkezi — asfalt:** real city asphalt, fine dark-grey aggregate with small light pebbles,
   slightly worn and faded, a few tiny cracks, very subtle sun-bleached patches.
2. **Sanayi — beton:** poured industrial concrete floor, light grey, fine sand grain, faint hairline
   cracks, two or three very faint old oil stains, light trowel marks.
3. **Liman — ıslak taş:** wet grey-blue cut-stone quay pavement, irregular stones tightly packed,
   damp sheen with soft reflections, tiny puddle spots between stones, a little salt residue.
4. **Avm — mermer:** polished cream/beige marble floor, soft natural veins, gentle glossy sheen,
   no tile joints (one continuous slab look), very clean.
5. **Gokdelen — koyu taş:** dark charcoal polished granite, fine white and grey speckles, subtle cool
   reflection like a plaza at night, very smooth.

İsteğe bağlı (daha da gerçekçi dursun diye): her zeminden ikinci bir sürüm iste:
> Same texture, but add very subtle real-world wear: faint dust, tiny scuff marks, a few small stains.
> Keep it seamless and calm.
İkisini birlikte oyunda deneriz, hangisi güzel durursa onu seçeriz.

Not: ChatGPT bazen kenarları dikişsiz yapmıyor. Önemli değil, dikişleri ben düzeltirim.
Çözünürlük: ne kadar büyük olursa o kadar iyi (en az 1024×1024).
