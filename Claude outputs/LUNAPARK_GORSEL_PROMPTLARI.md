## A) Harita arka planları (dikey, 2:3)

Her birinin başına bu ortak girişi yapıştır:

> Top-down bird's-eye view illustration for a mobile game map background, vertical portrait 2:3.
> Cozy, warm, hand-painted stylized look with soft lighting and gentle shadows, like a premium casual game.
> The CENTER of the image (about 60% of the width) must stay EMPTY and calm (plain ground only), because
> game level buttons and a winding path will be placed there. All details and props only along the LEFT
> and RIGHT edges. No text, no letters, no logos, no people, no UI.

**1. Giriş Kapısı (HaritaGiris)**
> Ground: light cobblestone plaza with a few confetti pieces. Edges: a colorful striped amusement park
> entrance arch seen from above at the top edge, a ticket booth, balloon cart with bunches of balloons,
> cotton candy stand, popcorn cart, flower planters, string lights between poles. Bright morning light.

**2. Çarpışan Arabalar (HaritaCarpisan)**
> Ground: smooth dark rubber floor with faint scuff marks. Edges: a bumper car arena with thick padded
> rubber walls in red and yellow, a few empty bumper cars parked at the sides, a mesh ceiling pole grid,
> traffic-cone style barriers, a token machine. Warm colorful indoor light.

**3. Dönme Dolap (HaritaDonmeDolap)**
> Ground: pale wooden boardwalk planks. Edges: the base and lower cabins of a big ferris wheel on one
> side, a small carousel roof with horses seen from above on the other side, a moving conveyor walkway
> strip along one edge, benches, lamp posts, ice cream cart. Golden afternoon light.

**4. Oyun Sokağı (HaritaOyunSokagi)**
> Ground: light pastel checkered tiles in cream and mint, a few scattered tickets. Edges: carnival game
> booths with striped awnings: a ring toss with bottles, a duck pond with little rubber ducks floating
> in a round pool, a can knockdown stall with soft balls, a claw machine full of plush toys, a pinball
> cabinet, shelves of teddy bears and prizes, a strength-test bell tower. Cheerful afternoon light.

**5. Sirk Çadırı (HaritaSirk)**
> Ground: red and gold circus ring floor with sawdust near the edges. Edges: the inside of a big striped
> circus tent seen from above, tiered wooden seating, a drum, juggling pins, a unicycle, a pedestal
> with a star, spotlights making warm pools of light. Evening, magical warm spotlight glow.

## B) Oyun zeminleri — GERÇEKÇİ (oyunda misketlerin altında görünen yer)

Her birinin başına bu ortak girişi yapıştır:

> Photorealistic seamless tileable ground texture, shot from directly above with a camera held parallel
> to the ground (perfect orthographic top-down, no perspective). Square 1:1, very high detail, real
> material photographed in soft overcast daylight: natural micro-detail, tiny imperfections, subtle
> color variation, NO strong shadows, NO objects, NO text, NO painted lines, NO borders, NO vignette.
> Must tile seamlessly on all four sides. The surface should be calm enough that a white chalk circle
> and small glass marbles stay clearly visible on top of it.

1. **Giris — renkli parke taşı:** small square paving stones in soft muted terracotta, cream and
   sage tones, tight joints, a few tiny faded confetti flecks.
2. **Carpisan — kauçuk zemin:** dark charcoal rubber sports flooring with fine colored EPDM speckles
   (red, yellow, blue), slightly matte, faint scuff marks.
3. **DonmeDolap — ahşap iskele:** weathered light wooden boardwalk planks running horizontally,
   natural grain, small nail heads, gentle sun-bleached color.
4. **OyunSokagi — pastel karo:** soft cream and mint checkered ceramic floor tiles, slightly worn,
   thin grout lines, tiny scuffs, a few faint paper ticket marks, calm and matte.
5. **Sirk — sirk halısı:** worn deep red felt carpet with very subtle gold thread texture, soft pile,
   a little sawdust dust, no pattern.

İsteğe bağlı ikinci sürüm (daha gerçekçi):
> Same texture, but add very subtle real-world wear: faint dust, tiny scuff marks, a few small stains.
> Keep it seamless and calm.
